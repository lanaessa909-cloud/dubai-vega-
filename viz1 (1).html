2. Encoding

The visualization has two linked views.

  • View 1 – map (choropleth). Each Dubai Municipality community is a shape (geoshape, Mercator projection). Colour encodes the change in median sale price per m² between 2019–2020 (before the boom) and Jan 2025 – Aug 2026, on a diverging blue–orange scale centred on 0%: orange = prices went up, blue = prices went down. Communities with fewer than 10 sales in either period are mid-grey (growth not reliable); communities with no unit/villa sales are light grey. The selected community gets a black outline.
  • View 2 – line chart. x-axis = quarter (time), y-axis = median sale price per m² (AED), colour = off-plan (orange) vs existing (blue) properties, point size = number of sales in that quarter. Quarters with fewer than 5 sales are left out because their median is not reliable. The label above the chart says which community is shown.

3. Interaction

  • Hover a community: tooltip with name, number of sales, median price before and now, and the % change.
  • Click a community: the line chart is filtered to that community only (a point selection on comm_num drives a filter in View 2).
  • Double-click: clears the selection, and the line chart shows all of Dubai again.
  • Hover a point in the line chart: quarter, type, median price and number of sales.

4. Value added

  • Parts 1 and 2 showed where prices are high. This view shows where prices moved, which is what an investor asks: which communities gained most since before the boom, and which barely moved.
  • The linked trend shows when the growth happened and whether it came from off-plan or ready homes in that community, so a buyer can see if off-plan is priced above or below ready homes in the same place, and whether the trend is still rising or flattening.
  • Greying out communities with too few sales keeps the reader from trusting a % change based on 2 or 3 sales.
